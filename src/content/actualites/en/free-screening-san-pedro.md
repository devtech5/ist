Alongside its general assembly held on Thursday 27 June 2019, International Santé pour Tous organised an awareness and free screening day for hypertension, diabetes and hepatitis in the city of San-Pédro.

The event took place on Friday 28 June 2019 at the organisation's head office in Bardot 18, with the support of the SCILIFE laboratory, represented locally by the Pharm-Eden agency.

## Prevention rather than treatment

The aim was to make people aware of the risks attached to these conditions and of the preventive measures available, in particular the need for a healthy lifestyle and a varied, balanced diet.

The organisation's president recalled the observation behind the initiative: many cases of hypertension and diabetes are recorded in San-Pédro, while preventive measures remain largely unknown. The point was therefore to bring residents in to have their blood sugar and blood pressure checked.

## A large turnout

From the earliest hours the stands were busy, especially with older residents. Several people who came stressed how much free access to these tests matters for households on modest incomes.

The departmental health director, present at the event, warned about the complications of these conditions, recalling that hypertension and diabetes often develop together and can lead to kidney failure. He insisted on following dietary advice and keeping medical appointments after a positive screening.

## Results of the day

In total, **307 people were screened**:

- 55 cases of hypertension
- 39 cases of diabetes
- 6 cases of hepatitis B
- 3 cases of hepatitis C

Everyone who tested positive was referred for medical care.
